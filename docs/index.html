unashamedly

AI-generated, on purpose, out loud.

I had ideas, I described them, and an AI built them. These are the ones that live on the web. The name is the disclaimer.

Lighthouses of New Zealand
An animated night chart of 86 coastal lights, each sweeping or flashing to its published characteristic on one shared clock. No land is drawn; the coast appears only where the beams stop.
single HTML file · canvas · ray-traced beams
Te Awa Kairangi Roadworks
The weekly Melling / Te Wai Takamori o Te Awa Kairangi roadworks email for Lower Hutt, redrawn as a map: what is closed, one-way, under traffic management or being resurfaced overnight, week by week, with what changed since last week.
single HTML file · svg · updated from each Friday's email
